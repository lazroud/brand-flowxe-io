Usage

Please follow these rules to keep the FLOWXE brand consistent across all touchpoints.

✓ Do

  • Scale proportionally
  • Place on solid brand colors from the palette
  • Use the monochrome version for single-color printing
  • Maintain clear space equal to half the mark's height
  • Use SVG whenever the medium supports it

✗ Don't

  • Change proportions, rotate, skew, or mirror
  • Replace the wave gradient with a flat color (except mono)
  • Add shadows, outlines, glows, or other effects
  • Place the logo on noisy backgrounds without proper contrast
  • Translate the wordmark — FLOWXE stays in Latin script

Typography
